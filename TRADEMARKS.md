# The Fledgetone name and logo

Fledgetone's code is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)). That licence lets anyone use, copy, change and share the code. It doesn't
give permission to use the **Fledgetone** name or logo as the name or logo of something else.

**You can, without asking:**

- Use the name to talk about Fledgetone: in articles, videos, tutorials, reviews and lessons.
- Say that your project is "based on Fledgetone", "a fork of Fledgetone" or "works with Fledgetone".
- Ship unmodified copies of Fledgetone under its own name, with its logo.
- Make music with Fledgetone and say so. Music you make is yours.

**Please don't, without asking:**

- Publish a changed version under the Fledgetone name, or with the Fledgetone logo (the bird
  with sound bars in `src/assets/fledgetone.png`, `public/favicon.svg` and
  `public/apple-touch-icon.png`).
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your project, product or service is the official
  Fledgetone, or is endorsed by it.
- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to Fledgetone's.

If you fork Fledgetone, change the name in `index.html`, `package.json` and the app's title and
about text, and replace the logo files above. Your fork is still under the GPL, of course: keep
the licence and the copyright notices.

Not sure whether something's fine? Open an issue and ask.
