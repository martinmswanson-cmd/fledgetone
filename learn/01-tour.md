Fledgetone is a whole music studio in a browser tab. In ten minutes you'll open a finished song, play it, take it apart and put it back together, and know where everything lives. Open [the studio](https://fledgetone.com/app/) in another tab or window and follow along.

### Open a song

The quickest way to learn a studio is to open something that already sounds good. Fledgetone comes with 76 **starters**: short songs in every style, ready to play and change.

1. Open the **File** menu at the top left and choose **New…**.
2. Type *boss* in the search box and click **Boss Fight**.
3. Press **Space** to play. Press it again to pause.

{{shot:tour-file-menu}}

> Browsers only allow sound after you click something. If you hear nothing, click **START AUDIO** in the top-right corner (pressing Play also starts it).

### The timeline

The big area in the middle is the **timeline**: the song from left to right, one **track** per row. Each track plays one instrument, and the coloured blocks on it are **clips**, short pieces of music the instrument plays. The red line is the **playhead**, where the song is now.

{{shot:tour-timeline}}

Try these while the song plays:

- Click **M** on a track's header to **mute** it, and **S** to **solo** it (hear it alone). Click again to undo.
- Click anywhere on the ruler (the bar numbers along the top) to jump there.
- Drag the **VOL** slider on a track's header to make it louder or quieter.

### Look inside a clip

Double-click any clip. Its notes open in the **piano roll** under the timeline: time goes left to right, pitch goes up the keyboard on the left. Every coloured bar is a note. Drag one up or down and press Space to hear the change.

{{shot:tour-piano-roll}}

> Made a mess? **Ctrl+Z** (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes it, as many steps as you like.

### The bottom panel

Under the timeline is the **bottom panel**, with tabs for the **Mixer**, the **Launcher**, **EQ & Dynamics**, **Game**, **Video** and **Sound FX**. Click **Mixer**: every track has a channel strip with its fader, and the master on the right sets the level of the whole song.

{{shot:tour-mixer}}

### The menus and search

Every tool lives in one of the menus along the top, laid out like a desktop studio: **File** to open, save and export, **Song** for arranging, **Track** for the selected track, **Tools**, **Video**, **Game**, **View**, **Learn** and **Help**.

You don't have to know where anything is, though. Press **Ctrl+K** (Cmd+K) and type what you're after: *reverb*, *export*, *violin*, *metronome*. Search finds tools, settings, instruments, starters and the guide.

{{shot:tour-search}}

### Your work saves itself

Fledgetone saves the song in your browser as you work. Close the tab and come back, and a banner offers to **Restore** it. To keep a song for good, or move it to another computer, use **File › Save**: it's a small file you can open again with **File › Open**.
