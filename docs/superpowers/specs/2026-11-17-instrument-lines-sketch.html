Instrument lines, a throwaway sketch

A made-up five-part song, not a Fledgetone project. Each instrument draws a line whose height and colour follow the pitch it plays, with a glowing dot at its head.

  • Stopping: when an instrument is silent longer than the slider allows, its line ends and its dot stays behind at the end.
  • Coming back: a new line rises from the bottom and reaches the pitch as the first note sounds.
  • Short rests: anything under the allowance keeps the line going flat, so it doesn't flicker. Drag the slider to 0 to see the jitter it prevents.
  • Additions to try: the four tick boxes each turn one idea on or off, so you can judge them one at a time.
  • Still guessed: an instrument playing a chord follows its top note.
