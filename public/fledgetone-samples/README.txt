Recordings for Fledgetone's "(Real)" instruments, converted to MP3.
How they were made: scripts/real-samples/README.md in the Fledgetone source.

From VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (CC0 1.0, public domain):
  cello-sustain, cello-spiccato, violins-sustain, violins-spiccato, violas-sustain, violas-spiccato,
  strings-pizzicato, strings-tremolo, solo-violin, harp, flute, flute-staccato, oboe, clarinet,
  bassoon, horn, horn-staccato, trumpet, trumpet-staccato, trombone, trombone-staccato, tuba,
  marimba, xylophone, glockenspiel, pipe-organ, chapel-organ
  upright-piano: sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio, redistributable as part of VSCO 2 CE

From Karoryfer Lecolds (CC0 1.0, public domain):
  guitar-black, guitar-green: Black And Green Guitars, recorded by Brian Wood
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars
  guitar-flatwound: Emily Guitar, played by D. Smolken
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar
  alto-sax: Weresax  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.weresax
  baritone-sax: Bear Sax  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.bear-sax
  double-bass, upright-bass: Meatbass, played by Drogomir Smolken
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.meatbass
  electric-bass: Growlybass  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.growlybass

grand-piano: Splendid Grand Piano, public-domain samples by AKAI
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano
