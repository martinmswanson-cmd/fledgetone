Recordings for Fledgetone's "(Real)" instruments, converted to MP3.
How they were made: scripts/real-samples/README.md in the Fledgetone source.

From VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (CC0 1.0, public domain):
  cello-sustain, cello-spiccato, violins-sustain, violins-spiccato, violas-sustain, violas-spiccato,
  strings-pizzicato, strings-tremolo, solo-violin, harp, flute, flute-staccato, oboe, clarinet,
  bassoon, horn, horn-staccato, trumpet, trumpet-staccato, trombone, trombone-staccato, tuba,
  marimba, xylophone, glockenspiel, pipe-organ, chapel-organ, muted-trumpet
  upright-piano: sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio, redistributable as part of VSCO 2 CE

From Karoryfer Lecolds (CC0 1.0, public domain):
  guitar-black, guitar-green: Black And Green Guitars, recorded by Brian Wood
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars
  guitar-flatwound: Emily Guitar, played by D. Smolken
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar
  alto-sax: Weresax  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.weresax
  baritone-sax: Bear Sax  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.bear-sax
  double-bass, upright-bass: Meatbass, played by Drogomir Smolken
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.meatbass
  electric-bass: Growlybass  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.growlybass

grand-piano: Splendid Grand Piano, public-domain samples by AKAI
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano

From the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios
  https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL (CC0 1.0, public domain):
  timpani, tubular-bells, vibraphone, harpsichord, harpsichord-release, piano-release, kalimba,
  tenor-sax, harmonica, ocarina, zither, concert-bass-drum, recorder

celesta: A Sampled Celesta by Neil Bickford (audio CC0 1.0, public domain)
  https://gitlab.com/echoparallax/a-sampled-celesta

Vocal chops (scripts/vocal-chops/build.py: cut into syllables, tuned onto the keys), all CC0 1.0
(public domain), each licence checked on the recording's own page:
  chops-soul: the short sung "a" of the legato vocal tutorial by Karoryfer Samples

  chops-airy, chops-shout: voice recordings from OpenGameArt (each page's licence: CC0)
    cicifyre, Voice Clip Packs for Visual Novels and RPGs (Bright Female, Mature Female)
      https://opengameart.org/content/voice-clip-packs-for-visual-novels-and-rpgs
    mirceakitsune, Dialog vocal samples  https://opengameart.org/content/dialog-vocal-samples
    nocturnalvanguard (AuraVoice), OoOoOoOoOoOoOo  https://opengameart.org/content/oooooooooooooo
    nocturnalvanguard (AuraVoice), Ghostly Humming  https://opengameart.org/content/ghostly-humming
    nocturnalvanguard (AuraVoice), Cheers  https://opengameart.org/content/cheers-0
    nocturnalvanguard (AuraVoice), oh alright!  https://opengameart.org/content/oh-alright
    ignasd, Au yeah!  https://opengameart.org/content/au-yeah
    emopreben, Greeting sounds  https://opengameart.org/content/greeting-sounds-by-emopreben
    Kenney, Voiceover Pack (40+ lines)  https://opengameart.org/content/voiceover-pack-40-lines
