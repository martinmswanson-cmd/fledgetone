For content creators

Your own music, ready to post.

Make the music for your videos, streams and beats yourself, then export it the way you need it: a showcase video of your notes, the exact length for a clip, a version to talk over, and kits for your stream and your beat store.

Showcase videosShow people how you made it

Showcase

A video of your song, made in one click

File › Showcase video exports your song as an MP4 where every note slides past a red playhead in its track's colour and lights up as it plays, with your title, the waveform and the time. Tall for phones or wide for everything else.

Smooth 60 fps and made at a high quality, so it stays sharp after a video site compresses it again. Hide whatever you don't want shown, from the title to the waveform; mute a track and it's left out of the picture too.

  • Portrait 9:16 or widescreen 16:9
  • Your own title and the line under it
  • One roll for every track, or a lane each
  • Or put your music on your own video

Lengths

Every length you need

Cut to length makes 6, 15, 30, 60 and 90-second versions of a song for an ad, a short or a story. It leaves out or repeats whole sections, so every edit falls where the music changes anyway, and it always ends on the song's own ending.

It can nudge the tempo a little so the version lasts exactly as long as you need, to a hundredth of a second.

  • Keeps the parts that matter: the chorus, the drop
  • Every edit on a bar line
  • One Undo brings the whole song back

Under your voiceMusic that makes room for you

Talking, singing or narrating over your music? Export it the way your video needs it.

A version to talk over

Export the song with an underscore version next to it: the same music without the lead melody, so it sits under your voice. A no-drums version too, for a calmer moment.

Dips when you speak

Load your own video and Dip under dialogue listens for voices and turns the music down for each line, then back up after. You can see and move every dip.

The right loudness

Match the level to where it's going: Streaming (-14 LUFS, the level YouTube plays at), Podcast and voice-over (-16 LUFS) or Loud for club music.

On your own video

Put the music on your MP4, where it sits on the timeline, and keep the video's own sound under it if you like.

Any format

WAV, MP3 or Ogg, the whole song or just the loop, stems for every track, or a link that opens your song in Fledgetone.

Sound effects too

Whooshes, risers, hits, notification pings and ambience from the sound effects maker, in the same project as your music.

Streams and beatsKits for your channel and your store

Channel kit

Music for your whole stream, from one song

File › Channel kit makes an intro and an outro for your videos, seamless loops for your stream's Starting soon, Be right back and Ending screens, three short alerts for a follow, a subscribe and a big moment, and a transition whoosh, all from the same song so they belong together.

The loops repeat with no gap in your streaming software, and every file is a 48 kHz WAV at one matched level, so you set the volume once.

  • Intro and outro
  • Starting soon, Be right back and Ending loops
  • Three alerts and a transition whoosh

Beat store

Everything you need to sell a beat

File › Beat store package makes a tagged MP3 preview for your store page and socials, the clean WAV and the stems (track-outs) for buyers, and an info file, all named with the title, tempo and key.

Record your producer tag once (Song › Arrange a beat › Producer tag) and it's laid over the preview at the start and as often as you like, with the beat dipping under it.

  • Tagged MP3 preview
  • Untagged 24-bit WAV and stems
  • Named with the tempo and key, cover image optional

Pocket

Start on your phone, finish on your computer

Open fledgetone.com on a phone and you get Pocket: tap in a beat on pads, add chords and a bass line, sing over it and share it. The same song opens in the full studio on a computer, as it is.

  • Pads, steps and a piano roll made for a thumb
  • Record your voice over the loop
  • An early version: the studio does more
