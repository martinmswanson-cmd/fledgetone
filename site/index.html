Music for games and video

Make music in your browser.

Fledgetone is a music studio that runs in a web page. Write songs, score video, and export music for games. There's nothing to install and no account to make, and your songs stay on your computer.

Fledgetone is laid out like a video editor, with tracks and clips on a timeline. It's made for people who write music for games and video, and for beginners. Open one of the starter songs and press Space to hear it.

Scoring to video

Line your music up with your video.

Load a video and it appears on the timeline, with a preview window that plays along. Fledgetone finds the cuts, marks each one, and can change the tempo so every cut lands on a bar.

Scored in Fledgetone

Two films, two kinds of score

Each film was loaded into the studio, its cuts found and the tempo fitted to them. The music was then written, mixed and exported in Fledgetone.

Open a downloaded project in the studio with File › Open.

  • Timecode23.976 to 60 fps, including 29.97 drop-frame.
  • Short versionsMake 6, 15, 30, 60 and 90-second versions of a song.
  • Lower under dialogueTurns the music down while people talk in the video.
  • ExportSend markers to Premiere, Resolve or Final Cut, or put the music on your MP4.

Game music

Music that changes as you play.

Each section of a song can be a game state. Try it in a game puts your song in a small 3D level, so you can hear it switch states, build up and play stingers while you play.

  • States and intensitySections loop on their own. An Intensity slider adds and removes layers.
  • Transitions and stingersChoose how states change, and add short cues in the song's key.
  • Unity, Godot and webExport a game kit with a player and an example scene.
  • FMOD and WwiseExport a package with scripts that set up the events.

FeaturesWriting and mixing

Write

Piano roll

Write notes on a grid, add chords in a key, and use the tools to add a bass line, drums and fills. Switch to sheet music to print a part or export MusicXML.

  • Scales, chords, arpeggios and swing
  • Mod wheel, vibrato and legato
  • Record from a MIDI keyboard or a microphone

Mix

Mixer and effects

Each track has its own effects, sends to reverb and delay, and automation. Group tracks into buses and finish on the master channel.

  • 644 built-in effects and instruments
  • Load Web Audio Module plugins
  • Freeze tracks to save CPU

Sound effects

Make coins, jumps, hits, menu clicks and about 70 other kinds of game sounds.

198 instruments

Synths, EDM basses, drum kits, and recorded pianos, guitars, strings, brass and winds.

Export

WAV, MP3, Ogg, stems, MP4 video and game loops. You can also share a song as a link.
