Why FledgetoneA real studio. Not an AI generator.

Fledgetone doesn't write songs for you from a sentence. It's a digital audio workstation, the same kind of tool producers and composers use: tracks, clips, a piano roll, a mixer and effects. The music that comes out is the music you made.

  • Every note is yours

    Draw notes in the piano roll, play them on a MIDI keyboard, or record your voice and instruments with a microphone.

  • Change anything

    Every note, sound, effect and fader can be edited and undone, and you save the whole project, not just an audio file.

  • Helpers, not autopilot

    Chord progressions, a drummer and fills follow plain music rules. They're a starting point you choose, change or delete.

  • Learn as you go

    Starter songs to pull apart, step-by-step lessons and a full guide teach you how music is put together.

  • Nothing leaves your computer

    No prompts, no uploads and no account. Your songs are saved in your browser and to files you keep.

Showcase videos

Show people how you made it.

Export your song as a video ready to post: your notes slide past the playhead track by track, in each track's colour, with your title, the waveform and the time. Pick tall for phones or wide for everything else.

  • Smooth 60 fps, sharp enough to stay clean after a site compresses it again
  • Your own title and the line under it; hide what you don't want
  • A small Fledgetone mark, nothing more
  • Or put your music on your own video, or share the song as a link

Scoring to video

Line your music up with your video.

Load a video and it appears on the timeline, with a preview window that plays along. Fledgetone finds the cuts, marks each one, and can change the tempo so every cut lands on a bar.

Two films, two kinds of score

Each film was loaded into the studio, its cuts found and the tempo fitted to them. The music was then written, mixed and exported in Fledgetone.

Open a downloaded project in the studio with File › Open.

Cut to length

One song, every length

Make 6, 15, 30, 60 and 90-second versions of a song for an ad, a trailer or a social clip, in a few clicks. Fledgetone leaves out or repeats whole sections, so every edit falls on a bar line where the music changes anyway. It always ends on the song's own ending and keeps the parts that matter most, like the chorus and the drop. It can nudge the tempo a little so the version lasts exactly as long as you need, to a hundredth of a second.

Game music

Music that changes as you play.

Each section of a song can be a game state. Try it in a game drops your song into a game right in the studio, so you can hear it switch states, build up and play stingers while you play. Pick a 3D arena, a 2D platformer or a rhythm game.

  • States and intensitySections loop on their own. An Intensity slider adds and removes layers.
  • Transitions and stingersChoose how states change, and add short cues in the song's key.
  • Unity, Godot and webExport a game kit with a player and an example scene.
  • FMOD and WwiseExport a package with scripts that set up the events.

FeaturesWriting and mixing

Write

Piano roll

Write notes on a grid, add chords in a key, and use the tools to add a bass line, drums and fills. Switch to sheet music to print a part or export MusicXML.

  • Scales, chords, arpeggios and swing
  • Mod wheel, vibrato and legato
  • Record from a MIDI keyboard or a microphone

Mix

Mixer and effects

Each track has its own effects, sends to reverb and delay, and automation. Group tracks into buses and finish on the master channel.

  • 645 built-in effects and instruments
  • Load Web Audio Module plugins
  • Freeze tracks to save CPU

Pocket

On your phone too

Open fledgetone.com on a phone and you get Pocket: the whole song as a grid of sections and tracks, with pads, steps and a piano roll made for one thumb. The same song opens in the full studio on a computer, as it is.

  • Tap a cell to play its instrument
  • Record drums and notes as the loop plays
  • An early version: the studio does more

DownloadInstall Fledgetone as an app

The installed app is the same studio, in its own window. It's free, takes a few seconds, and needs no installer or account. Your browser installs it and keeps it up to date.

  • Your work is kept safe

    A browser can clear a website's saved data when your disk gets full. The installed app asks it not to, so your autosave, sound library and recordings stay put.

  • Works offline

    Once installed, the studio opens without the internet. Instruments you've played before are kept too; new ones download the first time you use them.

  • Private

    Nothing is uploaded. Your songs and recordings live on your computer, not on a server, and there's no account to sign in to.

  • Its own window

    Open it from your dock, Start menu or home screen like any other app, with no tabs or address bar in the way.

  • Updates when you're ready

    New versions download in the background. The app tells you when one is ready and only switches when you click Reload, never in the middle of your work.

Keep saving important songs to files too (File › Save). To remove the app, open its window menu and choose Uninstall, or remove it like any other app.
